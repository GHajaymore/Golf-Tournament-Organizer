# Feature sweeps, from 2026-10-10

The eleven tournament probes ran three consecutive clean rounds on 2026-10-10 (33 of 33). They test
RUNNING a tournament. Everything else gets a sweep by class, scripted where it can be, and the
sweep set is re-run until it is clean three times in a row. This file is the worklist and the
record: what each sweep found, what was fixed, and what waits on a decision.

A finding is listed as it was reported. **"Confirmed" here means confirmed by reading all three
sites, not by running anything** — verify before fixing, and write the failing test first.

## Sweep 1 — what the screen OFFERS, the server ACCEPTS, and the app then READS

Three lists that must be the same list. The model case: the theme picker drew main colours from
`ACCENT_PRESETS` while the save (`isThemeKey`) and the stylesheet (`themeFor`) read a shorter one,
so Fairway and the Parkland scheme sat in the picker for two months and could not be saved (#864).
The sweep's method was checked against that case on the pre-fix commit before anything else.

Status: ☐ open · ☑ fixed (PR) · ◇ needs Ajay's decision · ✕ checked, not a defect
