## Sweep 10 — security, the front door and access (2026-10-11)

Three new instruments, each in `e2e/`:

- **`every-action-vs-a-stranger.spec.ts`** calls every server action the build exports (263; four
  are kept out because they spend the course directory's daily quota) by a plain POST, as three
  callers: signed out; the Commissioner of ANOTHER club carrying the seeded club's real ids in every
  argument that could be one; and a member of the club pointing at OTHER members' rows, in the live
  medal and in a match-play tournament. After every call it diffs every row in the database against
  the rows the seed made, reads the answer for anything of the club's, and puts the club back.
  Arguments are built from each action's signature, read by the TypeScript checker. Lists with
  reasons: `OPEN_DOORS`, `MAY_CHANGE_OWN`.
- **`front-door.spec.ts`** is one person coming in by the forms — sign-up, sign-out, wrong
  password, forgot, reset, a `next` pointing off the site, lockout. Every other spec arrives
  already signed in, so none of this had ever been operated by a test.
- **The every-screen walk** now also asks whether a label belongs to a button instead of its box,
  and whether a player or a passer-by is sent another member's email address in a page's data.
