# Live round: if something goes wrong

For the Tournament Director and the Organizer's support person. Print this page and keep it with the
paper cards. Fill in the blanks **before the event**.

**Call first:** Operations Lead `<name and number>` · Backup `<name and number>` · Owner `<name and number>`

**Rule one: do not stop play.** Players keep playing and keep their scores. Nothing is lost by waiting.
**Rule two: nobody deletes, resets or re-enters anything** until the Operations Lead says so.

## Who to tell, and what to say

Tell the Operations Lead by phone (not email). Say: **the time, what you see, how many people, and what you
have tried.** Example: "2:10 pm. Scoring will not open on five phones in two groups. Another phone on mobile
data opens it. We have not touched anything."

## Check first (two minutes)

1. Is it one phone, one group, or everyone?
2. Try a second phone on mobile data (not the course wifi). Open `tourneyhq.club/api/health`. It should say
   `"ok":true`. If it does, the app is up and the problem is signal or that phone.
3. Look at the hole marks on a scorer's phone: **Sent** is safe. **On your phone** is kept but not yet at
   the club. **Not sent** has a reason written beside it.

## The three most likely problems

**1. A phone cannot send its scores (no signal, or "Not sent").**
- Tell them: **keep playing, keep the app open.** Scores are saved on the phone and send by themselves
  when there is signal.
- Do **not** let anyone clear the browser, reload the page, sign out, use private browsing, or enter the
  same card on a different phone.
- At the finish, anything still **On your phone**: walk to the best signal (the clubhouse) and wait for
  "Sent". If it will not send, the player shows the phone to you and signs a paper card. Write "phone
  holes unsent" on it.
- "Not sent" with a reason (for example the round is locked): read the reason out. It needs a committee
  decision, not another try.

**2. The app will not open or the scoreboard is wrong for everybody.**
- Open the health page (check 2 above). If it fails on mobile data too, tell the Operations Lead now.
- Players with the scoring screen already open can carry on. Tell them **not to reload.**
- If groups cannot get scores in and the Operations Lead has not given a fix within **10 minutes**
  (the Owner's ruling, 4 Nov 2026), **start the paper fallback** below. The scoreboard may be out of date
  until scores are entered; tell the field the standings are delayed.

**3. Somebody cannot get in.**
- A player using a round code: check the code on the sign, and that they typed it at `tourneyhq.club/play`.
- A name that is not on the list or an email that is refused: tell the Operations Lead. Do not create a
  second entry for the same person; it makes two cards.
- Give them a paper card meanwhile.

## Keep the round going on paper

Have these ready **before the first tee time**: the printed scorecards (Organizer: Reports, Exports,
Print kit, one card per player or per group), a few blank spares, pens, and a clipboard at the scoring table.

1. Each marker keeps the card on paper. Marker and player **sign it** as usual; the signed card is the record.
2. Scores come to the table at the finish. The table keeps a simple list: group, time in, "signed".
3. When the app is back, the Organizer enters each card in the console (Entry: hole by hole or full card),
   in the order they came in.
4. If a player's phone also has holes "On your phone", look at the phone **before** entering the paper
   card. If the two differ, the app asks which to keep. **The signed paper card decides.**
5. Say aloud to the field that final results wait until every card is in.

## After

Write down: the time it began, the time it ended, who you told, what you did, and whether any card was
late or unsigned. Give it to the Operations Lead the same day. Do not photograph or post players' names
or scores outside the club's own channels.
