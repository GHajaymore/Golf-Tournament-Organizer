## IN FLIGHT: item 4, team and pair score entry

`docs/requirement-team-score-entry.md` is the decision and it is settled. The
rule: **the input mirrors the physical scorecard; the override may reduce
detail, it may never invent it.**

### Done

- **`src/lib/domain/team-entry.ts`** — the rule, derived from the format's
  `ball` rather than a new field, so a format added tomorrow is answered the
  day it is added. `teamEntryChoices`, `resolveTeamEntry`, `sideOnlyCost`.
  14 tests, including a sweep asserting the rule against `ball` for every
  format in the catalogue.
- **The control**, on the round card in `RoundTeamScoring`. A shared-ball
  round states "one ball, one card" rather than offering a dropdown of one.
  The cost of `side-only` sits beside the control and only once it is chosen.
- **No migration.** `Stage.scoreInput` holds both axes because a round is
  either a match round or a team round, never both — and both resolvers are
  TOTAL, so each falls back to its own natural shape rather than returning
  something a screen cannot render. Asserted in `team-entry.test.ts`;
  `setStageScoreInput` validates against whichever axis the format has.

### Next, and read this before starting it

**`TeamEntryClient` does not yet read the resolved mode.** A four-ball still
collects whatever it collected before, so the setting is currently honoured on
the Rounds screen and nowhere else.

That work is **a scoring change, not a UI one**, and it is the reason it was
stopped here rather than pushed through at the end of a long session:

- `match-cards.ts` deliberately refuses to credit a side's card to a player —
  *"a side's card is not a player's card — a foursomes pair returns one card
  for two people, and crediting it to either of them individually would invent
  a round neither played."* The requirement is the decision to revisit that
  comment for the two-ball formats only.
- If per-player four-ball entry starts feeding **individual handicap records**,
  that changes what `memberHandicapRecord` counts. Under WHS a four-ball score
  IS acceptable for handicapping when the player's own ball is recorded, so
  this is correct — but it must be deliberate, and `sideOnlyCost` already
  promises the club the opposite when they choose team-only.
- Per CLAUDE.md this is the combinations class: add cells to
  `matrix.test.ts` for format x stage type, field sizes from ONE, and assert
  against Rule 22 (foursomes) and Rule 23 (four-ball).

## Two open questions nobody has answered

- **Promoting a waitlisted member** into the field is not possible from the
  roster. They are correctly not "addable" — they already have an entry — and
  the action lives on Registration & field. Fine unless a club's real workflow
  is "review the queue on the roster and promote from there".
- **Soft cap and hard cap** are not implemented in the club handicap. They
  limit upward movement against a Low Handicap Index over 365 days, which needs
  a stored history that does not exist. `handicap-record.ts` says so at the top.
