## The decisions

1. **A new round is played off handicap (Net)** by default (`newRoundBasis`); it was gross. Six
   scratch-event specs now tap Gross. Stableford is net either way.
2. **Each round type offers only the formats it can be scored in** (`formatChoicesFor`, via
   `roundShapeMismatch`), and the server refuses the rest. **Skins** became card-only (no Skins
   knockout or round robin). **Alternate Shot** is no longer offered (it is foursomes) but a round set
   to it keeps it. Look-alikes carry a few words ("Four-Ball — pairs, the better ball counts").
3. **Picking a round type preselects its usual format** (Stroke play round → Stroke Play; round
   robin, bracket, single match → Match Play; team session → Four-Ball).
4. **Gross / Net / Both is on the face of the round**, with the allowance line under it.
5. **Customize this round** holds: how scores are recorded, the completion deadline ("When scores are
   due"), the sign-up deadline (leagues), the tiebreakers, one-round handicaps. Its summary says only
   what was changed, or "Nothing changed".
6. **Tiebreakers named apart**: "1. If a match is all square after the last hole" and "2. If players
   finish level in the table" (round robins); a stroke round states its countback in words.
7. **"On the day"** (Is this round over? and the scoring window) and the Played/Active chip appear
   only once the tournament is launched, or the round has a result or is closed.
8. **"Before the next round"** (carry-forward, the cut, Generate the next round) is on the face of a
   round robin, not in Customize.
9. **The knockout's draw** (One bracket / Main + plate / Two flights / Several flights) is chosen on
   the bracket round's own card as well as the Bracket screen. Main + plate's blurb: "The first round
   is the qualifying match: winners go on in the main draw, losers drop into a plate".
10. **A knockout added after a stroke round qualifies the top 16 overall** (it was top 2 per flight);
    after round-robin groups it stays per flight. "Per flight" is disabled only when exactly one flight
    is drawn.
11. **Every round** (from three playing rounds): one line saying what all share, one place to change
    format, holes and handicap for every unplayed round; rows then say only what differs.
12. **Notices folded**: missing course card, undated rounds, approximate handicaps, scoring mismatch,
    unchecked card — one card ("To sort out"), one line each, the reason a tap away.
13. **The drafting ("AjAi — coming soon") panel** is not drawn on Rounds & formats unless the club has
    drafting (it is off on every plan).
14. **The setup strip's "Now"** marks the step on screen, not the first unfinished step.
15. **The current round before any date has come**: an undated Round 1 is no longer skipped for a
    dated Round 2 (`currentDatedRoundIndex`).
16. **The Rounds & formats setup step, and launch, refuse a round that cannot be scored** in its format.
17. **Rounds & formats stays before Registration** in the guided order, not enforced.
18. **The cut's sentence** says it ranks on the total, and states a rule while nobody is entered.
19. **Registration's switches** (mobile number required, auto-confirm vs approve) show a tap at once
    (optimistic), falling back if the server refuses.

For each: agree, change, or reverse — with the reason, in your role's terms, and anything the
decision breaks or misses. Then any decision in #888 not listed here that you would have made
differently.

## What the panel said, and what was decided

Agreed by all four, kept: 2, 3, 4, 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18.

**Changed**

- **1, net default** — golf pro, tournament director and QA manager independently found that a round
  added after another ignored its gross/net, so Round 2 of a scratch championship came out net.
  FIXED: a new round repeats the last round's basis; net is only the first round's default; audit
  test both ways. Net stays the first-round default (golf pro: right for club medals, Stablefords,
  handicap knockouts and most team events). QA: net match play needs a course card where gross did
  not — say so when the round is added, not at launch.
- **10, overall qualification** — overwrote a per-flight choice and merged flighted championships.
  FIXED: only for the first knockout on an unflighted field; audit test. Open: 16 against a smaller
  field; saying how a tie at the qualifying line is settled.
- **7, On the day** — per round now: shown once the round's day has come (or it has a result or is
  closed), not on a round three weeks away.
- **Captions with an info button** (UI/UX) — thirteen labels pressed their own ⓘ; fixed app-wide
  with a guard.
- **9, plate wording** (golf pro, tournament director): "Lose in the first round and you go into the
  plate, a knockout of its own"; the guide too.
- **5 / 8, what is on the face** (tournament director): the play-by date and a league's weekly
  sign-up belong on the face of knockout, round-robin and league rounds; the tiebreakers apply to
  every round robin and must say so.
- **11, Every round** (UI/UX, QA, tournament director): skip a round whose day has come; show the
  rounds' own format; report refusals truthfully.
- **19, Registration switches** (QA): the actions THROW, and inside an async transition React sends
  that to the error screen — the old `void` swallowed it. Catch and say it at the switch.
- **Smaller, all taken**: notices sorted blockers first; SaveState out of accessible names; radio
  groups named; QualControl's radio names unique per card; "Foursomes (alternate shot)" and the
  greensomes/Chapman hints say "choose one ball"; the strip's colour follows Now; the draw picker
  not a card inside a card; Customize no longer opens itself for the carry question; stale "Result
  calculation" copy; the countback sentence read from `countbackSegments`; the guide's "Before the
  next round".

**Ruled**

- A Nassau on a stroke play round: nothing in the app creates matches for a round with no
  opponents, so the gate refusing it is right; the seeded festival writes its matches directly.

**Open, recorded** (golf): countback in net play taking 1/2, 1/3, 1/6 of the handicap (Committee
Procedures 5A(6)) — to be checked against the engine; a halved round-robin match defaulting to half a
point each; one prize per player under Both; a plate for non-qualifiers (17th–32nd) as well as
first-round losers; gross Stableford.
