## 2. The money model — designed in conversation, built here

The shape came from the product owner over the session. Recorded because the
reasoning is not recoverable from the code alone.

### Organization kinds (`lib/domain/org-profile.ts`)

Four kinds — `club`, `course`, `community`, `personal` — each declaring what it
MEANS: `sharedRoster`, `ledger`, `seasonPlay`, `ownsCourse`, `tracksCash`.
Everything asks this rather than comparing a string, so adding a kind is one
row and the compiler finds every place that needs an answer.

**A club and a course do not track cash.** The shop takes the entry fee, the
2s pot comes out of it, and the professional pays the winner — the app's job
there is to say who won. This was the product owner's own answer and it is the
flag that decides whether "take their money" and "you owe" appear at all.

### Money modes (`lib/domain/money-mode.ts`)

`none` · `float` · `split`, resolved **tournament → club → kind**. Both stored
columns default to empty meaning "follow the level above", so every existing
organization behaves exactly as it did until somebody chooses.

Per tournament, not per club: a society runs a thirty-pound Sunday roll-up and
a three-day trip with a minibus in the same season.

**Float and split are different questions.** Float money has left the players'
hands (one pot belonging to the tournament); split money has not (somebody
fronted it and is owed). Conflating them is how a settle-up ends up telling a
player they owe the organizer money they paid at signup. The float is
deliberately **not per player** — per-player fee reconciliation is a separate
feature nobody has asked for yet.

### Pot entry modes (`lib/domain/pot-entry.ts`)

`opt-in` (a name is in because somebody put it there) and `opt-out` (everyone
playing is in unless they said otherwise). Per pot, because a club runs both on
the same day: the closest-to-the-pin is on for everybody and the sweep is for
whoever fancies it.

One honest relaxation, confined to opt-out: **a player with no row counts as a
confirmed stake**, because the premise of "everyone is in" is a club that
collects as a matter of course. An organizer who has not been paid marks that
player unpaid.

`excluded` is a second column and not a reuse of `confirmed`: "took himself
out" and "has not paid yet" settle differently.

### Payouts are round-based, and final only

Money is won on a given day by a given card. A running skins position is not an
early view of the answer but a different number that looks like one — the carry
means one hole can take the lot. Rounds finish three ways: every card in, every
match settled, or the organizer closing the tournament.

## 3b. The §5 capability gaps taken on — 2026-08-18

Four of the audit's §5 gaps, built once sections 1–4 were clear. All four
follow the same shape, which is worth copying for the rest: a pure domain
module that **resolves rather than stores**, refusing with an explanation
rather than guessing, then actions, then UI.

- **Stroke-play countback.** The standard ladder — last 9, 6, 3, then the
  last hole; nine-hole rounds start at the last six. Read off the LAST
  round's card, on the basis the competition was played on. A tie that
  survives every step stays a tie: players share the position and the next
  is numbered past them, which is what tells a committee to run a play-off.
  An incomplete card never loses on countback. Live on the board.
- **Single Match Stage.** The stage was in the picker and did nothing. It
  needed no new engine — a single match is a round robin of one — only a
  pairing. Stores the RULE: first against second, winner of one round
  against winner of another, or two named players; resolved when the round
  opens. Domain, actions, picker, rendered.
- **Third-place match.** Fed by LOSERS, which is why it could not be another
  bracket round: `drawBrackets` accepts no loser-derived pairing except
  `plate`. Derived from the semi-finals instead, deliberately outside
  `buildBracket`. Off by default. Domain, actions, toggle, rendered. The
  match is marked `round: 0`, which nothing else uses — that is what stops a
  second one being created and keeps it out of the draw.
- **P2, P3, P4** closed the same day; see the audit doc.

Neither the Single Match picker nor the third-place toggle has been SEEN
rendering — the dev session had expired for one, and the demo tournament has
no Bracket Stage for the other. Both typecheck and build. Worth a look
before trusting them.
