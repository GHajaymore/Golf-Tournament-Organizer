# TourneyHQ brand guide

One page. Approved by Ajay on 25 Oct 2026 as a firm item. Every figure below was read from the source on 4 Nov 2026; where a line says "arriving with #1017" the code is on an unmerged branch. When the source and this page disagree, the source wins and this page is wrong: fix it.

## The name

Always **TourneyHQ**: one word, capital T, capital H, capital Q. Never "Tourney HQ", "Tourneyhq", "TourneyHq" or "Tourney-HQ". The wordmark is drawn as "Tourney" plus an "HQ" chip, which is a drawing, not a spelling: in running text and in every title it is plain "TourneyHQ". `src/lib/__tests__/brand-consistency.test.ts` holds the mark and wordmark to one copy each; `brand.test.ts` and `share-card-brand.test.ts` cover the rest.

## The mark and the wordmark

- **The mark** is "holing out": a flag on a pin, a filled cup, a green ball breaking the near rim. One drawing in `src/components/Logo.tsx` (the flagstick geometry `M18.6 6.1 V23.1` may appear in no other `.tsx`; the test above enforces it). Two treatments: the **flat mark** (the app, favicon, every generated icon) and the **emblem** (`emblem` prop: the mark in a dark-green disc with a brass ring; display use only, such as marketing, and never at icon sizes because the ring and dashed putt trail turn to mush at 16 px).
- **The wordmark** is `src/components/BrandMark.tsx`: "Tourney" in the heading face with an orange foil gradient (`.brand-mark` in `src/app/globals.css`), plus a solid orange "HQ" chip (`.brand-hq`). The two together are the **lockup**, `src/components/Lockup.tsx`; every lockup in the app goes through it.
- **Files.** `src/app/icon.svg` and `src/app/favicon.ico` (browser tab); `public/icon-192.png`, `public/icon-512.png`, `public/icon-maskable-512.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (installed app; listed in `src/app/manifest.ts`); `public/email/tourneyhq-lockup.png` (email). The icons are generated by `scripts/gen-icons.mjs`, which carries its own copy of the geometry; `brand-consistency.test.ts` keeps it in step. Do not hand-edit a PNG: change the generator.
- **Sizes.** Use the `LOGO_SIZE` steps in `Logo.tsx` (sm 19, md 22, lg 28, and the share-card sizes 44 and 96) and never a number of your own. The mark sits beside the wordmark at `MARK_TO_WORDMARK` = 1.45 (emblem 1.27) via `markSizeFor`. The mark is built to survive 16 px (a browser tab); the "HQ" chip is floored at 10 px.
- **Clear space.** Not written down in the source. Working rule for print, taken from the lockup's own gap: leave at least the height of the "H" in HQ clear on every side. (A rule proposed here, not yet in code.)
- **Never:** stretch or squash it; recolour it (the flag and ball are TourneyHQ's orange and green at every club, never a club's accent, decided 2026-09-18); redraw it or add a second copy of its geometry; outline the cup (it is filled so it survives small sizes); put it on a busy photograph; put the emblem's ring on an icon; set "TourneyHQ" in a face of your own instead of using the lockup.

## Colour

- **Brand colours** (`:root` in `src/app/globals.css`, `--thq-*`; nothing a club sets reaches them): flag orange `#f2862e` (light `#f6a566`, deep `#d06f23`), ball green `#56c74c`, chip text `#16181a`. On a light ground the wordmark alone takes the deeper orange stops `#c4611a`, `#b35512`, `#9c4a0f` (decided 2026-09-27), because the lighter three measured 1.8 to 3.1:1 on paper. The icon generator holds the same flag and ball values.
- **Emblem only:** disc `#0c1f0a`, ring brass `#c6a052` (`Logo.tsx`).
- **The two grounds** (`src/lib/themes.ts`). Dark, `DARK_GROUND`: page `#16181a`, card `#21231f`, text `#e9e9ed`. Light, `LIGHT_GROUND`: page `#f4f2ee` (paper, not clinical white), card `#fffefb`, text `#1a1c1e`. The ramp reverses between grounds: low steps are always foreground, high steps always background.
- **Tokens.** Components read `--color-*` custom properties and never hard-code a colour. One club theme (`themeCss`) drives the console, the public board and the player app. The scoreboard and the brand are the exceptions: fixed at every club.
- **Contrast.** Scores are read on a phone in sun. `SUNLIGHT_RATIO` is **7:1 on the dark ground and 4.5:1 on the light** (`themes.ts`). `sunlightVerdict` grades the ground a theme actually renders; `auto` is graded as dark.
- **No Pakistan-flag green.** Ajay's rule (memory note "No Pakistan-flag green"). In the source it appears as the green sweep: TourneyHQ's green is the India-flag green `#138808` (hue 115, a leaf green); the deep, blue-leaning greens (Fairway 151, Ivy 158, Signal 137) read beside white as another country's flag and were taken out on 2026-10-10. `greens-are-leaf-greens.test.ts` bans any colour in hue 128 to 172 (saturation 0.18 and up) from all source. It matters because the product is used in several countries and a green that reads as a national flag says something nobody chose to say (the reason as Ajay gave it on 2026-10-10 was that those greens looked like another country's flag; the source never names Pakistan). A club's theme is the club's choice: it can pick its own palette (and a club that types its own colour is outside that test), but the brand's own surfaces never take a club's colour, and the brand never takes the banned green.
- **The landing page's palette is its own and is read-only to this guide.** `src/lib/landing-palette.ts` (midnight navy, hue 223, since 2026-10-11) and `src/lib/landing/home-styles.ts`; two sections are green on a phone as Ajay's sunlight test. Do not extend, restyle or remove them on anybody's word but his. See `docs/claude/landing-page.md`.

## Type

Set in `src/app/layout.tsx` and `src/app/fonts.ts`; no face is fetched from a font CDN (`font-hosts.test.ts`).
- **Body and UI: Geist Sans** (variable; `--font-body`), with true tabular figures so score columns lock. **Numbers and code: Geist Mono** (`--font-mono`).
- **Headings and the wordmark: Fraunces** (variable weight axis, upright and italic; `--font-heading`, weights 500, 600 and 700 in use; the wordmark is 700). A serif belongs to the game's engraved honours boards, so it stays in headings: never in a score column.
- **The scoreboard: Oswald** (variable, 200 to 700; `--font-board`), only where a scoreboard is hung.
- **The landing's headlines:** Fraunces cut for display (`--font-poster`), weight 560, set in `home-styles.ts`.

## The QR mark (arriving with #1017)

Described from `src/lib/qr.ts` on `claude/scoring-by-group-2a`, not yet on `main`. A printed QR (a round's signs) carries the TourneyHQ mark in its centre.
- **Error correction H** when the mark is drawn (30% repairable); M for a code with no mark, so it stays coarse.
- **At most 15% of the code's area** (`LOGO_AREA = 0.15`, half of what H repairs, so the other half is left for scuffs, rain and folds). The centre box is the largest odd module count under that, with **one module of white all round**, and never nearer a corner than the finder pattern.
- **Four-module quiet zone** round the code (ISO/IEC 18004).
- **The wordmark goes under the code, never inside it**, on signs **30 mm and wider** (`QR_WORDMARK_MIN_MM = 30`, `showsWordmark`), in the brand colours. A scorecard's small corner code carries no name. (Ajay, 2026-11-05.)

## Pictures

- **Real app screens only.** Never an AI-made picture of the app. Landing pictures are unedited captures (`docs/claude/landing-page.md`).
- **Vector first** (SVG or CSS). Raster at least 2x the screen size, and at least 300 dpi for print.
- **Text is never baked into an image**: it stays live text so it scales, translates and reads crisp.
- **Samples show an invented demo club and invented people**, with `@example.invalid` addresses. Never a real member, club or tournament name (Hard rules 1 and 2: the repository is public).

## Voice

Plain, human, a golfer talking to a golfer. "Side game", never "bet" (`public-pages-never-say-bet.test.ts` sweeps all of `src`).
